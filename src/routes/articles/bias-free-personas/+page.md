---
title: "Bias-free personas: build profiles that help instead of mislead"
description: "Persona profiles drift into biography when they are written from opinion. Four practical steps to keep them useful and inclusive."
date: 2022-11-14
---

When we write persona profiles from personal opinion and experience, we tend to include a lot of the persona's biography. That biography is the part that ages badly, invites debate, and quietly reduces the value of the persona. Bentley University has written [a good explanation of the problem](https://www.bentley.edu/centers/user-experience-center/beware-persona-bias).

It is very hard to remove bias from persona profiles entirely. These four steps make them more usable and inclusive, and they are easy to apply in Personas for Jira.

## 1. Do not give personas real names

A real-sounding name invites a reader to fill in everything the profile does not say. Use the occupation, a preference, or a goal in the name field instead. "The music teacher", "The bookworm", and "The wine lover" describe a role without inventing a person.

## 2. Use an image that is not a real person

A photograph of a real person anchors the persona to appearance and demographics that the research may not support. Personas for Jira shows non-photographic images by default for this reason. You can still search the photo library if a real image genuinely helps your team, but it should be a deliberate choice.

## 3. Build the profile from real data

A profile driven by interviews, support tickets, and product data is harder to argue with and easier to act on. A profile driven by a workshop's assumptions is neither. Where the data does not exist yet, leave the field empty rather than filling it with a plausible guess.

## 4. Plan for people who differ from the majority

People with disabilities and other individual differences are often averaged away by a single "typical" persona. When a profile stops describing a real group well, create another persona rather than stretching the first one. Personas are cheap to add; a misleading profile is expensive to correct.

## Keep the profile beside the work

The point of a persona is not the profile document. It is the decisions the profile informs.
