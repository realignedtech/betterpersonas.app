---
title: "Building your persona"
description: "Create a persona profile in Jira with a name, description, photo or colour, then add custom fields for motivation, goals, blockers, and more."
---

After installation, the Personas for Jira app will be available in all Jira projects (all project types are supported). When you open the app, you will be prompted to create your first persona profile.

![Creating a persona in Personas for Jira](/docs/CreateScreen.jpg)

Specify a persona name and a brief description (optional) and select either a photo from the Unsplash library or a solid colour to represent the persona. This photo or colour will also be visible in issues linked to this persona.

Once you have created the persona, you can edit the basic fields provided by the app, which are currently "Motivation", "Goals" and "Blockers".

![Editing the persona profile fields](/docs/EditScreen.png)

You can add any field you need via the overview page - click "Fields" in the top right hand corner and add either a global field or a field scoped to the current project.

![Adding custom fields to persona profiles](/docs/EditFields.png)

After doing so, you can click on the persona in order to edit the newly available fields and complete the persona profile.
